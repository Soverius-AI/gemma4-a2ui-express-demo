## Gemma models

Gemma 4 is developed by Google DeepMind. The model weights are **not bundled in this repository**. Enabling an original model downloads the chosen checkpoint from Hugging Face; the optional WebLLM selection loads separately converted, statically hosted assets; review that checkpoint's model card, license and notice files before use or redistribution.

- Untuned ONNX model: <https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/gemma-4-E4B-it-ONNX>
